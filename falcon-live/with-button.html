Proud of our Past. Resolute in our Future.

Welcome to Falconians, the online gathering place for Falcon alumni from all corners of the globe.

Our mission is to reunite Falcon graduates, serve as a digital archive for cherished memorabilia, and act as a communication platform for reunions, college fundraising, and more.

Our site is designed to be user-friendly, searchable, and packed with engaging content. Certain sections are exclusive to registered Falcon alumni, ensuring a personalized experience. If you’re a Falcon graduate, we invite you to register and gain full access to these special areas.

Are You a Falconian?

  • Anyone who for any period of time attended the College as a pupil is automatically considered a Falconian.
  • Any academic or administrative staff member who has served at least five years at the College is automatically a Falconian.
  • The Head may nominate people to be Honorary Falconians. The nominations have to be approved by the Falconian Society Committee.
  • Lastly, the Committee may appoint someone a Falconian for outstanding service to the College or the Falconian Society.

Leadership

The Falconian Society is lead by a committee consisting of up to 13 members. Currently the committee members are:

Colin Bewes (Hervey 1978) - IT Manager
Michael Howe-Ely (George Grey 1968) - Regional Director- Sub-Saharan Director (ex_Zimbabwe)
Nathan Maertens (George Grey 1996) - Director-at-Large

Brian Darling (Hervey 1968) – President
Peter Coulson (Founders 1983) – Treasurer
Dave Kirkman (Chubb 1986) Headmaster
Tapiwa Mafi (George Grey 2003) – Regional Director Zimbabwe
Edward Sankey (Oates 1990) – Falcon Foundation Trustee
Martin Bashall (Tredgold 1972) – Falcon Foundation Trustee
