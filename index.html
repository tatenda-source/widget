— The Mechanics

How a gift
moves.

From the moment you pick a use case to the moment Falcon's bursar sees the cleared payment is roughly forty-five seconds for in-country mobile money and two business days for diaspora cards. There is no middleman holding your money.

01

Choose a use case and an amount.

Browse the six open campaigns. Read each ledger — what it funds, who approved it, what's already raised. Pick one. Set your amount in USD. Optionally leave a note for the donor wall.

02

Pay through Paynow's existing rails.

The widget hands off to Paynow — the merchant aggregator already trusted by thousands of Zimbabwean businesses. You complete the gift using whichever rail you already use.

EcocashOneMoneyInnBucks ZimswitchVisaMastercardPayPal
03

The school is paid. You are receipted. The ledger updates.

Funds settle directly to Falcon's institutional account — routed by Paynow against the specific campaign you chose. A receipt is emailed to you. The campaign's progress bar ticks up in real time. Reconciliation is automatic.

— The Bigger Story

Paynow for Schools.

What Falcon has here, every Zimbabwean institution should have. A programmable giving and fees layer built on the same Paynow merchant infrastructure the country's businesses already run on — embeddable on any school's existing site, no rebuild, no new merchant account, no spreadsheets in the bursar's inbox.

01

One merchant, many use cases.

Schools don't need a separate account per campaign. Paynow holds the merchant relationship; we route programmatically to named ledgers under one settlement.

02

Diaspora and local on one widget.

Card rails for offshore alumni, mobile money for in-country — all from a single embed. No DIY Stripe, no Western Union forms.

03

A ledger anyone can audit.

Every campaign shows raised, goal, donor count, and message wall in real time. Trust beats fundraising appeals.

04

Bursar-friendly by default.

Receipts, donor lists, and CSV exports are automated. The bursar runs school finance, not donation spreadsheets.
